About the Collection

Welcome to the creative space of Christian Rosales. This collection serves as a visual and structural showcase of my growth as a computing student at the University of Baguio. Driven by a deep curiosity for how users interact with digital environments, this project marks the beginning of my journey into frontend development and user interface design.

The design layout emphasizes clean structures, intentional spaces, and accessible layouts—core pillars of Human-Computer Interaction. By exploring different digital frameworks and coding standards, I aim to bridge the gap between technical functionality and visual appeal, ensuring every component serves a distinct purpose.

As you navigate through this collection, you will find snapshots of my academic milestones, programming milestones, and personal interests. I look forward to expanding this platform into a comprehensive portfolio that documents my skills, projects, and readiness for a professional path in the technology sector.
